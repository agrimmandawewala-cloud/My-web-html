Why do you clean water for age 10 - 20

Imagine This...

If all the water on Earth fit inside a 2-liter soda bottle: Almost the entire bottle is salty ocean water you can't drink. A small splash is frozen solid in Antarctica. The clean freshwater available for all 8 billion people on Earth would equal less than a single drop! Because our drinking water supply is so tiny, keeping it clean is a huge deal.

The 4 Big Reasons We Should Clean Water

Reason 1

Kills Invisible Germs Natural river or pond water might look clean, but it is often full of tiny "bugs" you can't see without a microscope: Bacteria & Viruses: They cause severe stomach bugs and make you sick for days. Parasites: Tiny critters that travel into your stomach and cause infections. Water plants use safe doses of chlorine or bright UV light to zap these germs before water reaches your tap.

Reason 2

Takes Out Chemical Pollutants & Heavy Metals Dirty water doesn't just hold germs—it carries hidden chemical waste: Pesticides washed off farm fields during rainstorms. Heavy Metals like lead that leak from old pipes or factories. "Forever Chemicals" used in waterproof gear and non-stick pans. Fun Fact: Boiling water kills germs, but boiling does NOT remove chemicals or lead. In fact, boiling pure water away leaves behind a higher concentration of chemicals! Water treatment facilities use heavy-duty carbon filters to trap those toxins.

Reason 3

Filters Out Trash and Microplastics When plastic bottles or bags break down in the sun, they turn into tiny specks called microplastics. Fish and water animals swallow these plastic bits by mistake. Cleaning water stops these plastic pieces from ending up in our food and drinking water.

Reason 4

Saves Fish and Ocean Life Cleaning water isn't just about what comes out of your tap it's also about what goes down your drain! When you shower, flush the toilet, or run the dishwasher, that dirty water (called wastewater) flows into sewers. If cities dumped untreated wastewater directly back into lakes and rivers, it would feed green algae, blocking sunlight and pulling all the oxygen out of the water. Without oxygen, fish and plants suffocate.Cleaning wastewater before sending it back into nature keeps aquatic life alive.
